\section*{Generated results}

Every figure below is produced by \texttt{cmd/bench}, \texttt{cmd/compare},
\texttt{cmd/diagnose} or \texttt{cmd/coverage} and inserted from a
\texttt{*.gen.tex} fragment. Recall is measured over the passages that were
actually in the generator's context, whose mean size is reported beside it,
because a multi-round architecture puts more than \texttt{-top-k} passages
there; the matched-budget rows (10 and 12 passages) are what make the
comparison fair. ``EM answered'' is Exact Match restricted to the questions
the system did not decline to answer.
